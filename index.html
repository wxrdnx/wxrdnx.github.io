About Me

I am an incoming PhD student in Computer Science at Purdue University. My main research area is in computer security, with my current research centering on binary analysis, reverse engineering, and vulnerability discovery. More broadly, I'm drawn to the "science of hacking": what it takes to reason rigorously, empirically, and systematically about the discovery, exploitation, and mitigation of vulnerabilities.
